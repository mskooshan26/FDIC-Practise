The three rules

$250,000, counted three ways

$250K

Per depositor

Each person is covered up to $250,000.

× each bank

Per insured bank

Money at a different FDIC bank gets its own separate $250,000.

× each category

Per ownership category

At the same bank, single, joint, retirement, trust and business accounts are each counted separately.

Covered (deposits)

  • Checking accounts
  • Savings accounts
  • Money Market Deposit Accounts (MMDAs)
  • Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
  • Cashier's checks and money orders from the bank

Not covered (investments)

  • Stocks and bonds
  • Mutual funds and ETFs
  • Annuities and life insurance
  • U.S. Treasury securities (backed by the government, not FDIC)
  • Cryptocurrency
